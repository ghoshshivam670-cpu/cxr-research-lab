# Contributing to CXR-Research Lab

Thank you for your interest in contributing to CXR-Research Lab!

This is an independent research initiative focused on reliable and explainable deep learning for chest X-ray analysis.

## Who Can Contribute?

We welcome students, developers, and researchers interested in:

- Python and PyTorch
- Deep Learning and Computer Vision
- Medical Image Analysis
- Explainable AI
- Data Science and Research Methodology

You do not need to be an expert. Consistency, curiosity, and a willingness to learn are important.

## Areas of Contribution

1. Dataset exploration and preprocessing
2. CNN architecture development
3. Model training and optimization
4. Evaluation and calibration
5. Grad-CAM explainability
6. Literature review and research documentation

## How to Get Started

1. Read the project README.
2. Explore the research objectives.
3. Open a GitHub Issue describing the contribution you would like to make.
4. Discuss your proposed changes before starting substantial work.
5. Submit a Pull Request with a clear explanation of your contribution.

## Research Principles

- Maintain reproducible experiments.
- Document methods and limitations.
- Never upload private or identifiable patient data.
- Do not present experimental results as clinical evidence.
- Cite relevant research and dataset sources.

## Collaboration

We believe good research comes from transparent communication, critical thinking, and shared learning.

Let's build and investigate together!

**Research. Experiment. Evaluate. Improve.**
